# Texas Septic Drainfield Sizing Reference

> **Quick answer:** TCEQ Chapter 285 §285.32 sets drainfield absorption area based on soil perc rate and daily design flow. Faster perc means smaller drainfield. Slower than 60 min/in is not conventional territory.

## The absorption area table

| Perc rate (min/in) | Absorption area (sq ft per 100 gpd) |
|--------------------|--------------------------------------|
| ≤5 | 100 |
| 5–15 | 165 |
| 15–30 | 250 |
| 30–60 | 330 |

## Trench geometry

- Width: 36 inches
- Depth: 18–36 inches (24" common)
- Lateral spacing: minimum 6 feet center-to-center
- Lateral length: typically capped at 100 feet per run
